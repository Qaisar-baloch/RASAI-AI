HOSPITAL SERVICES — PATIENT FAQ (SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMO)

OPD TIMINGS AND DOCUMENTS
The General OPD is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. New patients must bring
a valid CNIC/B-Form and, if available, previous prescriptions or lab reports. A new
patient registration slip is issued at the front desk before consultation.

DEPARTMENTS AVAILABLE
Cardiology: Monday, Wednesday, Friday, 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM.
Dermatology: Tuesday, Thursday, 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM.
Pediatrics: Daily except Sunday, 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM.
Orthopedics: Monday, Wednesday, 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM.

EMERGENCY SERVICES
The Emergency department operates 24/7 and does not require an appointment. Emergency
cases are triaged by severity, not by arrival order.

BILLING AND COMPLAINTS
Billing disputes (overcharge, duplicate charge, insurance claim issues) should be raised
at the Billing Help Desk with the original receipt. Disputes are typically resolved within
7 working days. For complaints about staff conduct or service quality, patients can file
a formal complaint with the Patient Relations Office, which responds within 3 working days.

APPOINTMENT POLICY
Appointments can be booked up to 2 weeks in advance. Patients who miss an appointment
without cancelling at least 2 hours in advance may be marked as a no-show, which can
affect priority for future same-day bookings.

LAB AND DIAGNOSTIC REPORTS
Routine lab reports (blood tests, urine tests) are available within 24 hours. Imaging
reports (X-ray, ultrasound) are available within 48 hours. Reports can be collected in
person or requested via a query ticket in this system.
