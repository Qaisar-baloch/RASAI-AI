UNIVERSITY & HEC STUDENT SERVICES — POLICY FAQ (SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMO)

TRANSCRIPT AND DEGREE ISSUANCE
Official transcripts are processed by the Registrar's Office and take 5-7 working days
from the date of request. Degree certificates for graduating students are issued within
60 days after the final result notification, pending HEC attestation. Students can track
transcript status by submitting a query ticket through this system with their roll number.

HEC ATTESTATION AND VERIFICATION
HEC (Higher Education Commission) degree attestation is required for students applying
abroad or for government jobs. The attestation process takes 10-15 working days once
the university has forwarded the degree to HEC. Students must first get an Inter-Board
Committee of Chairmen (IBCC) attestation for their intermediate certificate before HEC
attestation of a bachelor's degree.

SCHOLARSHIP VERIFICATION
HEC need-based and merit scholarships require the university to verify enrollment status
each semester. If a scholarship disbursement is delayed, the most common cause is a
pending verification from the university's Financial Aid Office. Students should first
check with the Financial Aid Office before escalating to HEC directly.

FEE COMPLAINTS
Fee-related complaints (overcharging, duplicate payment, late fine disputes) are handled
by the Finance Office. A refund or correction request, once submitted with a valid
payment receipt, is typically resolved within 10 working days.

EXAMINATION AND RESULT COMPLAINTS
Result recheck/remarking requests must be submitted within 15 days of result
announcement, along with the prescribed remarking fee. The Controller of Examinations
office processes remarking requests within 21 working days.

GENERAL COMPLAINT ESCALATION
If a complaint is not resolved by the relevant department within its stated timeline,
students may escalate to the University Ombudsperson's office, and for HEC-related
matters, directly to the HEC Complaint Cell.
